CALC: sd_from_se(se, n)                        <- converts standard error into SD
CALC: sd_change_r05(sd_baseline, sd_final)     <- imputes the SD of the change (r=0.5) when only baseline and final SDs exist
CALC: pool_dl_md(lists)                        <- DerSimonian-Laird pooled estimate; lists = [[m1,sd1,n1,m2,sd2,n2], ...] in trial order
