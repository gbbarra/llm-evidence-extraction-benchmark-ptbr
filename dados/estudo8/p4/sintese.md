# Synthesis — P4 EN-harness run (100% gemma4:12b)

The meta-analysis included 7 studies involving a total of 493 participants to evaluate the effect on HbA1c levels. The pooled mean difference (MD) was -0.5 (95% CI [-0.78, -0.22]), indicating that HbA1c levels fell more in the experimental arm. Significant heterogeneity was observed among the included studies ($I^2$ = 90.2%).

Individual study results varied in significance. The studies by Saslow 2017 (MD -0.5; 95% CI [-0.89, -0.11]), Dorans 2022 (MD -0.2; 95% CI [-0.29, -0.11]), Chen 2020 (MD -0.43; 95% CI [-0.8, -0.06]), Thomsen 2022 (MD -0.27; 95% CI [-0.45, -0.09]), and Goday 2016 (MD -1.8; 95% CI [-2.2, -1.4]) reported results that did not cross zero. In contrast, the results from Saslow 2023 (MD -0.18; 95% CI [-0.38, 0.02]) and Wang 2018 (MD -0.32; 95% CI [-0.87, 0.23]) crossed zero.
