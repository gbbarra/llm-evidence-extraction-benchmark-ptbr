# EXTRAI — Pre-registered protocol, Study 1

**Registered 2026-08-27, before any model run.** Later amendments only as dated sections at the end. General method: [`METHOD.md`](../../METHOD.md).

> *This is the English translation of the pre-registered protocol, which was originally written in Brazilian Portuguese. The original wording is preserved verbatim in the repository's git history (commits `3b282d0` through `ad29f95`). File names keep their Portuguese stems to preserve published links.*

## 1. Question

Do the four veteran models of the FIEL table extract evidence from primary clinical trials with the fidelity of human meta-analysis reviewers — and does the ranking between them invert relative to the writing ranking? The verdict of FIEL parts 13–14 ("the big models are extractors/auditors, not writers") becomes a testable hypothesis here.

## 2. Anchor meta-analysis

> Ashraf N, Zargar OUU, Albina A. **Comparison of Goal-Directed Fluid Therapy and Conventional Fluid Therapy in Elective Major Abdominal Surgery: A Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials.** Cureus, 2026-06-04. DOI [10.7759/cureus.110243](https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.110243) · [PMC13235771](https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC13235771) · CC BY 4.0.

Selected for: (a) publication after the four models' training cutoffs; (b) CC BY license; (c) 14 included RCTs, of which **8 with open-access full text** (the feasibility bar was ≥6); (d) 11 published tables covering the three tasks — baseline characteristics, intraoperative data, per-arm outcomes, 7-domain risk of bias, and GRADE; (e) a topic (perioperative hemodynamics) adjacent to the FIEL corpus (paper A2, cardiopulmonary bypass), preserving thematic continuity between the benchmarks.

## 4. Pre-existing inconsistencies of the anchor (registered BEFORE the runs)

Detected while building the corpus and frozen here so grading has no interpretive freedom. None was "fixed" in the answer key:

1. **RoB (table 1)** lists "Ramsingh et al. [21]" — a study **absent** from the characteristics table — and **omits** Weinberg et al. [39].
2. **Intraoperative (table 4)** lists "Koo et al. [37]" and "Peltoniemi et al. [38]" (also outside the characteristics; both CC BY) and **omits** Schmid et al. [44].
3. **Morbidity (table 5)** shows "**Yun** et al." with totals 39 (GDFT) / 36 (control), while the characteristics table gives **Yoon** et al. as 36 (GDFT) / 39 (control) — divergent spelling and possibly swapped arms.
4. Redondo Calvo et al.'s title appears in the journal record with the typo "Person**alizezed**" (preserved in citations).
5. **Sujatha et al.'s row in the characteristics table is corrupted**: the control ASA cell reads "2 days, 11:42:00" — a classic Excel time-formatting artifact where an ASA ratio should be; the GDFT n (200) corresponds to merging the FloTrac+PVI arms (100+100) of the three-arm trial, and the control n (101) does not appear literally in the primary's text.

Ghost-study rows (refs 21, 37, 38) score in no task. Each inconsistency is adjudicated against the primary sources during grading and, if confirmed, enters the anchor's errata file.

## 5. Tasks and form

### T1 — Structured extraction (per primary)

The model receives: the **perturbed** primary's full text (article first, instructions after — exploiting KV prefix caching) + the form below. It returns JSON, one object per field: `{"valor": "<as reported>", "onde": "<section/table of the article>"}`. Field not reported in the article: `{"valor": "NR"}`.

Fields (primary facts; mirroring the anchor's tables 3–11):

```
n_randomizados_gdft, n_randomizados_controle
tipo_cirurgia
laparoscopia_gdft, laparoscopia_controle            (as reported: count or %)
asa_gdft, asa_controle                              (I:II:III:IV ratio, as reported)
fluido_total_gdft, fluido_total_controle            (mL; mean±SD or median(IQR), as reported)
cristaloide_gdft, cristaloide_controle
coloide_gdft, coloide_controle
perda_sanguinea_gdft, perda_sanguinea_controle
uso_inotropico                                      (as reported)
morbidade_eventos_gdft, morbidade_eventos_controle  (n of patients with ≥1 complication)
mortalidade_gdft, mortalidade_controle              (in-hospital or 30-day deaths)
los_hospitalar_gdft, los_hospitalar_controle        (days, per arm)
tempo_flatus_gdft, tempo_flatus_controle
tempo_ingesta_oral_gdft, tempo_ingesta_oral_controle
tempo_evacuacao_gdft, tempo_evacuacao_controle
ileo_pos_op_gdft, ileo_pos_op_controle              (n of cases)
```

Out of scope (the reviewers' meta-analytic computations): RR, MD, 95% CI, weights, GRADE.

### T2 — Risk of bias (per primary)

Same perturbed article (same KV prefix), different instructions: judge the 7 Cochrane domains of the anchor's table 1 — sequence generation, allocation concealment, blinding of participants/personnel, blinding of outcome assessors, incomplete outcome data, selective reporting, other bias — plus the overall judgment. JSON output: `{"dominio": {"julgamento": "Low|High|Unclear", "justificativa": "<1 sentence>"}}`.

### T3 — Synthesis (per model)

The model receives **its own T1 extractions** (replicate 1 of the 8 primaries, without the articles' text) and writes, in Portuguese, a 250–400-word narrative synthesis of the evidence body: GDFT's effect on morbidity, mortality, length of stay and gut function, with direction, qualitative magnitude and uncertainty. Inventing numbers absent from the extractions is forbidden.

## 6. Perturbation (the dual reading proof)

- **K = 3 numbers per primary**, chosen among values that appear in the anchor's answer key (guaranteeing the corresponding form cell is hit), one per distinct region of the article when possible (abstract/methods/results).
- Magnitude: a 5–15% change of the value, plausible in context, not colliding with another real value of the article; same number of decimal places.
- All occurrences of the number in the text are altered consistently (including the primary's tables).
- The original↔perturbed table (`perturbacoes-estudo1.json`, local, outside the repository) stays **sealed until grading** and is published in full with the evaluation.
- At grading, a perturbed cell: perturbed value → scores normally ("read"); original published value → **recitation** (zeroes the cell, counts as contamination evidence); other cases → normal rules.

## 7. Scoring and tolerances (T1)

Labels and values per `METHOD.md`. Pre-registered tolerances for "exact":

- Rounding at the last decimal place the key reports (e.g., 3810.4 ≈ 3810).
- Explicit, correct unit conversion (L↔mL, h↔days).
- Equivalent ASA ratio format (e.g., "7:31:5:0" ≡ "I=7, II=31, III=5, IV=0").
- Mean±SD vs median(IQR): if key and model report different statistics, the cell goes to adjudication (the source decides which statistic the article actually reports).
- Key "NR" + a model value → mandatory adjudication (candidate disagreement-adjudicated-to-model: the reviewer may have missed the datum).

**Adjudication**: every non-exact cell passes through the rite (verify against the primary source before deducting), with the literal quote recorded in the grading file. Adjudicator: LLM judge (the same line as FIEL) + human author as tie-breaker.

## 9. Replicates and queue

- T1: 8 primaries × 4 models × **2 replicates** = 64 runs.
- T2: 8 × 4 × **2 replicates** = 64 runs.
- T3: 4 models × **1 replicate** = 4 runs.
- Queue order per (model, primary): T1r1 → T1r2 → T2r1 → T2r2 — the article precedes the instructions in the prompt, so all four runs reuse the KV prefix.
- Between-replicate agreement (share of identical cells r1 vs r2) is reported as a stability metric.

## 10. Pre-registered hypotheses

- **H1.1 (rank inversion)**: on T1 cell accuracy, the two big faithful models beat the two smaller ones — directional prediction: qwen3.8:27b ≥ gemma4:26b > gemma4:12b ≥ qwen3:14b. The extraction ranking does NOT repeat FIEL's writing ranking (where 12b > 26b > 14b > 27B on average).
- **H1.2 (reading, not recitation)**: zero recitation cells (original value in a perturbed cell) — the anchor is post-cutoff and the primaries, even the old ones, were not memorized with numeric precision.
- **H1.3 (rare invention)**: invention rate < 5% of cells in every model; omission will be more common than invention.
- **H1.4 (RoB in the human range)**: overall agreement with the reviewers between 60% and 90% of domains (the human inter-reviewer RoB agreement literature typically sits at 70–85%).
- **H1.5 (synthesis preserves direction)**: every model preserves the direction of effect on outcomes where its own extractions are correct; direction errors occur only downstream of wrong cells.
- **H1.6 (reverse audit)**: ≥1 disagreement will be adjudicated in the model's favor — that is, Study 1 will find at least one extraction error by the anchor's human reviewers (the section-4 inconsistencies suggest the review is not immaculate).

## 11. Reported metrics

1. Cell accuracy per model (primary), with exact/derivable/adjudicated decomposition.
2. Invention rate and omission rate, separately.
3. Reading proof: read / recited / absent, per model.
4. Overall and per-domain RoB agreement; between-replicate stability.
5. Synthesis verdict per outcome (direction preserved/inverted/invented).
6. Anchor errata found (H1.6), with source quotes.
7. Wall-clock time and tokens per run (extraction vs summarization economics).

## 12. What this study does NOT test

English prompts (the primaries are in English; the instructions, in Portuguese — the Brazilian researcher's scenario), other quantizations, models outside the FIEL table, and figure/forest-plot extraction (text and tables only). Each is a candidate future study.

## Amendments

### Amendment 1 — operational perturbation rules (2026-08-27, before any run)

Registered while building the perturbed copies, before any model read any text:

1. **Automatic selection with semantic anchors.** A key number is only eligible if all (round or 2-digit numbers) or at least one (numbers with ≥3 significant digits) of its text occurrences sit within ≤120 characters of a field anchor word (e.g., fluid→"fluid/volume/infused", morbidity→"complications"). Reason: the first automatic pass showed that short numbers ("19", "72", "200") collide with unrelated facts — other variables' percentages, measurement times, protocol boluses, even reference page ranges. Each key cell can be perturbed at most once; reference lists are removed from the text first.
2. **Documented manual curation.** Where the automatic pass falls short of K=3, additional values may be chosen by hand, with ALL occurrences inspected and recorded (file `perturbacoes-manuais.json`, sealed with the main table). Manual inspection replaces the automatic anchor.
3. **Primaries with no literal key value.** When no key cell exists literally in the text (the Sujatha case: the MA merged arms and derived/converted statistics), numbers that **feed form fields** are perturbed instead (e.g., per-arm crystalloids). For those values the recite-the-review check is void by construction (the number is not in the MA), but the reading check and the primary-memorization check remain active.
4. **K is a target, not a guarantee.** Studies may end with fewer than 3 perturbations when no safe values exist (the Schmid case: arm n's appear 14+ times in mixed contexts and no other numeric cell is usable). The final count per study is recorded in the seal.

### Amendment 2 — closed stratum: the 6 remaining primaries (2026-08-28, registered before any stratum run)

The author legally obtained the 6 paywalled primaries (refs 26, 29, 30, 33, 41, 47): PDFs through his own institutional access (Diaper, de Waal, Calvo-Vecino, Hokenek) and free legal author manuscripts located via NCBI/Europe PMC (Arslan-Carlon, Coeckelenbergh). Study 1 thereby covers **all 14 primaries — the entire meta-analysis**.

Stratum rules:
1. **Source files outside the repository** (copyright): `corpus/fechados-staging/`, `corpus/fechados-texto/` and `corpus/perturbados-fechados/` are not versioned. Third-party reproduction requires obtaining the articles by one's own means (DOIs in the protocol table); scripts and seals are published at grading.
2. **Identifiers**: each closed primary uses the pseudo-ID `REF<n>` (e.g., REF26) in all instruments, with the DOI/PMCID mapping recorded in the protocol.
3. **Text pipeline**: PDFs → text via pypdf with typographic-ligature normalization (ﬁ/ﬂ→fi/fl), line-break de-hyphenation and whitespace collapsing; the XML manuscript (Arslan-Carlon) follows the open-stratum flow. Stratum F0: measure sizes and confirm the 16,384 context.
4. **Perturbation with Amendment 3's corrected boundary**: unit-glued numbers ("4088mL") are reached; numeric-range hyphens (800-2750) are reached; letter-preceded hyphens (COVID-19) stay blocked. All other rules identical (anchors, distinctiveness, documented manual curation, seal closed until grading: `perturbacoes-fechados.json`).
5. **Symmetric queue**: the same 4 models, same frozen configurations, T1 and T2 ×2 replicates per primary (96 runs), in the same block order as the main queue.
6. **T3b — synthesis of 14**: at the end, each model writes a second synthesis over its extractions of all 14 primaries (4 runs), comparable to the 8-primary T3 — measuring whether more evidence changes the conclusion.
7. **Grading**: the same Amendment-4 pipeline (cell-by-cell source verification, extended official key, public adjudication); accuracies reported per stratum (open/closed) and pooled.

### Amendment 3 — two perturbation leaks; proof neutralized in two cells (2026-08-27, detected during block 2, before any grading)

An audit triggered by an apparent qwen3:14b "recitation" event revealed the failure was **the harness's, not the model's**: the substitution's regex boundary missed two occurrences of the original values, which remained in the perturbed text the models read:

1. **Weinberg (PMC5589093)**: "…2050mL (1199:2700) vs. **4088mL** (3400:4525)…" — the unit-glued number ("4088mL") escapes the `(?![\w.])` lookahead.
2. **Sun (PMC10694978)**: "…[1199 ml (**800-2750** ml)…" — the range hyphen triggers the guard created against "COVID-19"/reference pages, and the occurrence was not replaced.

Resulting rules, symmetric for the four models:
- In Weinberg's and Sun's `fluido_total_controle` cells (4088→4620; 2750→2906), the **"recited" verdict is unattributable** (the original was in the input). "Read" remains attributable when the model returns the perturbed value. These two cells leave H1.2's recitation denominator.
- The qwen3:14b episode (returning 4088 in both replicates) is recorded as **reading of inconsistent input**, not contamination.
- The perturbed corpus stays **frozen** as-is for all of Study 1 (fixing it mid-stream would break comparability between already-run and yet-to-run blocks). The boundary fix (allow unit-glue; distinguish numeric-range hyphens from acronym hyphens) applies to Amendment 2's closed stratum and to future studies.

*(Closed-stratum note, same class: in REF26/Diaper, the PDF split "113" into "1 13" in the abstract, leaving a readable original in the input; the corresponding cell was neutralized the same way. The de Waal/REF29 flowchart digits and the Hokenek/REF47 age-range collision led to documented exclusions in the closed seal — REF47 ended with zero perturbations, recorded.)*

### Amendment 4 — source-verified official answer key; primary metric redefined (2026-08-27, before any grading; at the author's request)

Reason: during blocks 1–3, three models independently extracted n=39/36 for Yoon, against the anchor characteristics table's 36/39 — and the primary source confirms the models ("The GDHT group (n = 39)… the control group (n = 36)"). The published meta-analysis contains extraction errors; using it as the final ruler would punish models for being right.

Rules:
1. **Two-layer answer key.** Layer 1 (`gabarito-ma.json`, as published) stays frozen and untouched. Layer 2 (`gabarito-oficial.json`) is built by verifying **every gradable cell against the original primary source**, with: the value as the source reports it, the **literal quote** that supports it, and the relation to the MA (literal / derivable / **MA-erratum** / MA-inferred / true-NR).
2. **Metrics redefined.** Study 1's primary metric becomes **accuracy against the source** (layer 2). **Agreement with the human reviewers** (layer 1) becomes secondary — and its explained divergences (MA errata) are reported as a result in their own right (H1.6).
3. **Verification method.** A public script (`verificar-gabarito.py`) locates each value in the source under pre-declared equivalences (count↔percentage via arm n; hours↔days; last-place rounding) and records candidate passages; the adjudicator (Claude, under the human author's supervision) confirms cell by cell. Cells without clear support stay "pending-adjudication" and score against no model.
4. **Independence disclosure.** Verification uses only the original texts and the MA — not the model outputs — but began after the adjudicator had seen partial outputs from blocks 1–3. Mitigation: every official-key cell carries a literal source quote, auditable by any third party; no cell is accepted without a passage.
5. Perturbations stay on top: a perturbed cell scores against the perturbed value (the "input truth" the model actually read), with the original recorded in layer 2.

*(Adjudicator errata registered under this amendment's rite: the withdrawn "Redondo swapped arms" claim — the primary contradicts itself, abstract vs body, and the MA was right; the withdrawn "Wu inotrope unsupported" claim — rigid search windows had hidden the source's vasoactive table; and the near-deduction of Hokenek's literal "40/40". All three on public record in the errata file.)*
