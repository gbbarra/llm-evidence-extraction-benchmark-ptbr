% ===== PROGRAMA =====
\paragraph{The ablation program: casts, committee, scaffolding.} The baseline result raised three questions that were answered by a pre-registered sequence of arms over \emph{identical} inputs (Stage E's sheets and the sealed seeds are shared, so every arm isolates exactly one design variable; a public question ledger orders them): can a single model carry the pipeline (cast ablations); is a multi-auditor committee a mechanism or an after-the-fact artifact (committee arm); and how much scaffolding makes small models close the tool loop (harness hardening).

\paragraph{Cast ablations: the all-12B pipeline.} Rerun with every stage on the extraction champion, the pipeline completed in 61 minutes (4.5$\times$ faster, everything on the integrated GPU) --- and inverted its answer. The 12B auditor behaved as a \emph{per-sheet anomaly gate} rather than a per-field verifier: sheets containing an arm swap received full scrutiny (both swaps caught and fixed with literal quotes, plus a transposed sample size), while sheets carrying only an isolated seed were confirmed wholesale --- 6/10 seeded cells caught (vs.\ 9/10) with \emph{zero} false alarms, and, auditing its own extraction, it confirmed its own two genuine errors 26/26 (correlated blindness in its purest form; notably, the ambiguous table that confused the baseline auditor confused this one \emph{identically}). The arithmetic stage collapsed: in both lanes the model wrote tool calls \emph{and} a final answer in the same output, executed nothing, and answered by head --- two of seven studies, both signs inverted, and a pooled $+0.85$ $[+0.31, +1.39]$ on the wrong side of zero. The synthesis then narrated the inverted pool as a statistically significant reduction. A second pre-registered cast (audit and arithmetic on the 14B model of the other family, still all-iGPU) landed in between --- 7/10 seeded cells caught with 7/7 exact corrections, including the two character-level seeds \emph{no other auditor caught}, by-head per-study estimates largely correct but both pooled estimates wrong --- and exposed a further finding: the three auditors' blind spots are \emph{complementary} --- no seed escaped all three, and the union of the 27B's and the 14B's flags covers 10/10, a detection ceiling whose test as an actual \emph{mechanism} follows below. The by-head casts also surfaced confabulated trial bylines that turned out to have been invented at \emph{extraction} --- the corpus builder strips the articles' title lines, and the extractor, facing a field its input could not answer, invented author names instead of abstaining; the ungraded field then passed every audit in every cast unflagged. Extraction excellence did not transfer to tool discipline: \textbf{casting stages by measured winners is a correctness requirement, not an optimization} --- and a field the ruler does not grade is a field no gate protects.

\paragraph{The committee as a mechanism.} Because the arms' audits are independent runs over identical seeded sheets, their verdicts can be combined legitimately --- but a committee is a combination \emph{rule}, and the retrospective table sets the trade-off space: the 27B$\cup$14B union flags 10/10 seeded cells at a 15.3\% false-alarm rate (above the pre-registered 10\% bound); majority-of-three flags 7/10 at 3.5\%; the intersection 6/10 at 0\%. A pre-registered arm then tested two rules \emph{end to end}, building committee-audited sheets mechanically and running the baseline calculator and synthesist once per rule. Under the union rule with champion-priority conflict resolution, \textbf{all ten seeded cells were repaired and the calculator's pooled estimate reproduced the committee sheets' mechanical truth digit for digit} ($-0.47$ $[-0.73, -0.21]$) --- the study's first arithmetically perfect end-to-end lane --- at the predicted false-alarm cost (the sheets' truth drifted from $-0.52$ to $-0.47$ as members' wrong corrections were imported). Under majority voting the sheets preserved the clean truth ($-0.52$) but the champion calculator itself answered by head in that run (zero executed calls, $-0.45$): a fourth independent demonstration that the tool loop is fragile even at the top of the field.

\paragraph{From spontaneous to scaffolded discipline.} The v1/v2 harness measured what models do \emph{unprompted} --- and only the 27B ever drove the calculator from inside the pipeline. A declared instrument evolution (harness v3, a separate module; every net a fixed string with a mechanical trigger) closes the four observed workflow modes: pending calls now execute before any final answer is accepted; a zero-call answer draws one reprompt to use the tool; pool rows inconsistent with the model's own per-study calls draw one reconciliation reprompt; the closure and call-as-data nets remain. A pre-registered championship --- all four models, both lanes, identical audited sheets, under v3 --- asks the program's final question: not \emph{whether} small models spontaneously orchestrate (they do not), but whether any of them can compute correctly \emph{when the harness holds them to the loop}. \emph{[Championship and fourth-auditor results pending; this paragraph receives them before submission.]}



% ===== DISCUSSAO =====
The ablation sharpened the picture: two model families implement ``audit'' with different mechanisms --- per-field verification versus per-sheet anomaly gating, at 90\%/6.5\% versus 60\%/0\% sensitivity/false-alarm trade-offs --- yet fell into the \emph{same} trap on the same ambiguous table: some blind spots are architectural, others belong to the document.
